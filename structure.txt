Suggested Design Direction

Think of a blend between an IT company and a facility management provider. The website should emphasize professionalism and reliability rather than technical complexity. Use large service cards with clear icons, plenty of whitespace, and prominent call-to-action buttons ("Request a Quote" and "Contact Us"). A blue-and-white color scheme with subtle animations works well for this type of business.

The overall impression should be that JN Market is a single trusted provider capable of handling IT support, office equipment, HVAC, and security systems for both businesses and individuals. This "all-in-one technical services" positioning can be reinforced throughout the page with messaging such as "One Partner for All Your Technical Needs."
